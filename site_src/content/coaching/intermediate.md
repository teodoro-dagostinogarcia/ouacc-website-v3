The intermediate range covers players who are roughly in the region from
handicap 4 to 14. Ian Plummer is happy to give tailored sessions to
individuals or groups of members. Please [contact
him](mailto:ian.plummer@balliol.ox.ac.uk) to arrange a session.

The notes below come from intermediate coaching sessions given by Robin
Brown to OUACC members.
