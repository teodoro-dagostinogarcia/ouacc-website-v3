Association croquet is easiest to learn by playing it. The Club offers introductory coaching and a welcoming route into the game, while the resources below help you continue learning between sessions.

## Learning the game

For an introduction to the game and its tactics, see [Ian Plummer's Oxford Croquet](https://www.oxfordcroquet.com/). Croquet England also provides the [basic laws](https://www.croquetengland.org.uk/) and the full Laws of Association Croquet.

You do not need to master the full laws before you start playing. A beginner can learn the fundamentals first and add detail as experience grows.

## Coaching

At the start of Trinity term, OUACC offers free introductory coaching for beginners. Sessions cover the basic rules, strokes and tactics, and the Club provides the equipment you need.

You do not need to be a member to attend the introductory sessions. Please wear flat-soled shoes.

## Joining OUACC

The quickest way to improve is to play regularly. Membership gives you access to the courts, Club equipment, coaching and internal and external competitions. See the [membership page](../oucc/join.html) for current information.

## Joining Croquet England

Croquet England is the national governing body for the sport in England. Membership gives access to tournaments and other opportunities beyond the University. See the [Croquet England website](https://www.croquetengland.org.uk/) for current membership information.
