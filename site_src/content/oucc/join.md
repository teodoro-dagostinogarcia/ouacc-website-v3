Membership gives you access to the Club's courts, equipment, coaching and playing programme. The Club welcomes students and experienced non-University players, subject to the current membership arrangements.

## General information

The Club uses two full-size courts in the University Parks. Members use an online court booking system and can access the Club hut and equipment under the Club's current arrangements.

All the equipment you need to play is kept at the hut. Please wear flat-soled shoes to protect the courts. Members should also read the [Croquet Byelaws](byelaws.html).

Once your membership is confirmed, you will receive the information you need for court booking, Club activities and any hut access you have requested.

## Membership options

The Club offers different membership durations to reflect the University calendar. Current fees and eligibility should be checked here before joining, especially outside term time.

A hut key may be available against a deposit. The key gives access to the hut and equipment outside Club evenings, subject to the Club's rules.

Experienced non-University members and alumni may also join, subject to availability and the University's limits on non-University membership. Once admitted, members take part on the same basis.

## Joining

To join, follow the current application process and provide the information requested by the Club. Please contact the Secretary if you have questions about eligibility or the application process.

## Further information

For practical questions, contact a member of the [Committee](committee.html).
