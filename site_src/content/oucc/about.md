The Club promotes association croquet within the University at every level. You can come for a social game, learn from other members, or compete at a high level.

Members have access to two full-size courts in the University Parks, together with Club equipment and coaching. A hut key can be provided against a deposit, and you only need flat-soled shoes to play.

The Club welcomes complete beginners as well as experienced players. We run coaching sessions, Club evenings and fixtures for a range of standards. Handicap play also allows players of different strengths to have competitive games together.

## Club evenings

Wednesday evenings are Club evenings, with members and committee members usually at the courts from 5 p.m. onwards. The arrangement continues through the summer vacation.

## Beginners' evenings

At the start of Trinity term, the Club runs free coaching sessions for beginners and non-members. Committee members and experienced players are available to help new players learn the basics and get comfortable on court.

## Membership

Membership is open to University and non-University players, subject to the Club's current arrangements and any University limits. See the [membership page](join.html) for current options, access arrangements and joining instructions.

## Fixtures

The Club's fixtures range from beginner-friendly matches to higher-level competition. Members are encouraged to enter fixtures at a level appropriate to their experience. Contact the [Captain](committee.html) if you would like to play.

## The Varsity Match

OUACC plays Cambridge University at the Hurlingham Club in London. The match combines singles and doubles and forms one of the Club's principal annual fixtures.

Croquet is a Half Blue sport at Oxford. The Club can recommend players for University sporting awards in line with the University's current criteria. See the [Half Blues page](../varsity/halfblue.html) for the historic list.

## Student Championships

The Student Championships are open to full-time students and include singles and doubles competition. See the [Student Championships page](../fixtures/studentchamps.html) for current information.
