The OUACC courts are in the University Parks, close to the centre of Oxford.

## From Parks Road

Enter the University Parks through the Keble Road entrance on Parks Road. Take the right-hand path and then keep right at the fork. The courts are on your right after about 240 metres.

## From the Linacre entrance

Enter through South Lodge and keep left for about 200 metres. The courts are on your left.

## Maps and location

[Google Maps](https://maps.google.com/?q=51.7601804,-1.2530324) and [Apple Maps](https://maps.apple.com/?ll=51.7601804,-1.2530324) can be used to find the courts.

The nearest postcode is OX1 3JA, for the South Lodge entrance on South Parks Road. There is no public parking in the Parks. Visiting-team parking can occasionally be arranged, depending on other events in the Parks. Please contact the Club Captain in advance.
