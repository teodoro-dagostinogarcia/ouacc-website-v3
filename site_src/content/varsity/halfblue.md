Croquet is a Half Blue sport in Oxford. Half Blues are currently awarded
to players who play off a handicap of 8 <sup>‡</sup> or lower at the
annual Varsity match, although these criteria have been different in the
past. Here we aim to maintain a record of all Half Blues awarded for
croquet. If you received a Half Blue and are not on the list, please let
us know.
