The Varsity Match is OUACC's annual competition against Cambridge University and one of the Club's principal fixtures.

The 2026 match was held at the Hurlingham Club in London on Monday 22 June. Cambridge won 5-4.

## Current match information

The current team, match arrangements and result should be the first point of reference each year. See the [Varsity archive](archive.html) for previous teams, results, reports and photographs.

## The Varsity tradition

The match combines singles and doubles and has been part of the Club's sporting life for generations. The surviving archive contains team photographs, reports and results dating back many decades.
