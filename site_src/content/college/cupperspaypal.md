<td colspan="2" class="bodytext"><p>The entry fee for Croquet Cuppers is
£4 PER TEAM. Team captains are responsible for paying their team's
fee.</p>
<p>In order to complete your entry for Croquet Cuppers 2026, please pay
the £4 fee via PayPal. This should generate an automatic confirmation
from PayPal, and all teams will be emailed by the Cuppers Director once
entries have closed ( <strong>Saturday 10pm on April 25 (0th Week TT)
2026</strong> .)</p>
<p>Any PayPal account or credit or debit card can be used so long as the
team captain's <em><strong>Oxford University</strong></em> email address
is in this box.</p>
