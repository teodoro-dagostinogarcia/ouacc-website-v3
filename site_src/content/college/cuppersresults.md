Please report each match result promptly so the draw can be updated and
the competition can continue on schedule.

## Report a result

Email the Cuppers Director with the information below.

- Your Oxford email address, if different from the address you are
  using.
- Winning team ID.
- Losing team ID.
- Score and any relevant comments.

<a
href="mailto:2026croquetcuppers@gmail.com?subject=RESULT%20-%20Cuppers%202026"
class="button button-solid">Email the result</a>

## Request an extension

Ask for an extension before the existing deadline. Include your team ID,
your opponents' team ID, the date requested, and the reason for the
request.

<a
href="mailto:2026croquetcuppers@gmail.com?subject=EXTENSION%20-%20Cuppers%202026"
class="button button-solid">Request an extension</a>

## Request a review

If a dispute requires a decision by the Cuppers Director, email the
Director with your team ID, your opponents' team ID, and a concise
explanation of the issue.

<a
href="mailto:2026croquetcuppers@gmail.com?subject=DISPUTE%20-%20Cuppers%202026"
class="button button-solid">Request a review</a>

## Contact the Cuppers Director

For any other Cuppers question, email <2026croquetcuppers@gmail.com>.
