1.  **What should we do if our college doesn't have a court?**
    - Play both games at your opponent's courts or use another college's
      courts.
2.  **What happens if the ball hits the peg?**
    - If the ball has not been through all twelve hoops, nothing
      happens - the game does not reset. See <a
      href="https://www.croquet.org.uk/?p=games/association/laws/7th#law:22"
      target="_blank">Law 22</a> for further details.
3.  **Which way does the ball go through the final hoop?**
    - Away from the peg. See diagram above
      <a href="https://www.croquet.org.uk/?p=games/association/laws/7th#law:1"
      target="_blank">Law 1</a> .
4.  **Where does the game start?**
    - The game starts from either baulk-line - not a mallet's length
      from the first hoop. See <a
      href="https://www.croquet.org.uk/?p=games/association/laws/7th#law:11"
      target="_blank">Law 11</a> .
5.  **When has a ball run a hoop?**
    - When the ball clears the plane of the playing side of the hoop and
      comes to rest. See <a
      href="https://www.croquet.org.uk/?p=games/association/laws/7th#law:20"
      target="_blank">Law 20</a> .
6.  **What happens if both teams have equal points after both games?
    E.g. 26-18 & 18-26**
    - You may choose to play a deciding game with any two players from
      your team or conduct a coin flip.
