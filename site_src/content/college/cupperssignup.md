We are delighted to welcome entries for Croquet Cuppers 2026. You will need a team of **4 players ALL from the SAME college/PPH** .

A large number of our entrants have never played croquet before, so don't be afraid to get stuck in if it is your first time.

The deadline for entries is **Sunday 26 April (1st Week TT) 2026** (i.e. first day of Trinity Term).

There are two simple steps to confirming your entry:

1.  **Fill out the <a href="https://forms.gle/2SRWXs3YpEFeTVDW9" target="_blank">contact details form</a>**  
      
    and  
2.  **Pay your £4 entry fee** through PayPal [here](cupperspaypal.html) , making sure to confirm your captain's Oxford email address.
